隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 20 日
适用于纸帐 · 分页手帐（Paper Journal）1.0。

1. 本机笔记与偏好

纸帐由 Yuchen Wang 提供。手写笔迹、输入的文字、图片和导入的 PDF 保存在设备的应用存储中。纸帐没有开发者运营的笔记服务器，不向开发者自动上传笔记内容，不包含广告、跟踪或第三方分析 SDK，无需注册纸帐账号。

外观、连续浏览和橡皮擦偏好保存在本应用本地设置中，不用于识别或跟踪用户。

2. iCloud 文档

设备已登录 Apple 账号，且系统允许纸帐使用 iCloud Drive 时，纸帐通过 Apple 的 iCloud 文档服务备份和同步可编辑原稿。文档使用用户本人的 iCloud 存储空间；开发者无法通过该服务读取用户的私人笔记。iCloud 的处理受 Apple 隐私政策及用户的系统设置约束。

未连接 iCloud 时，可以继续在本机记录。用户可在系统设置中管理纸帐的 iCloud 权限与存储，并可主动导出原稿及 PDF。

3. 导入、剪贴板与分享

纸帐处理用户主动导入、粘贴或创建的内容，不在后台读取剪贴板。导出、打印或分享由用户发起，保存位置、服务与接收方由用户选择；纸帐不会自动向其他人分享笔记。用户选择的文件提供者、键盘、打印或分享服务分别遵循自身的隐私政策。

4. 保留与删除

笔记保留至用户删除相应数据。书架删除操作会先把笔记放入「最近删除」，可以恢复；已有本地备份与 iCloud 历史版本也可能继续包含原稿。单纯移到最近删除不会彻底擦除所有副本。

需要清除全部数据时，请管理本机应用数据，并在系统 iCloud 存储管理中删除纸帐云端文档，同时自行删除已经导出或分享的副本。在此之前，请确认已保存需要保留的备份。

5. 诊断、支持与联系

纸帐没有自行发送统计或诊断数据的 SDK。Apple 是否处理系统诊断或崩溃信息取决于用户的系统设置和 Apple 的政策。用户主动发送支持邮件时，开发者仅将收到的联系信息和问题描述用于回复与解决问题；请勿提供私人笔记或不必要的敏感信息。

隐私或支持联系：jyuchenwang@gmail.com。本政策随功能变化更新时，会更新日期和说明。

Privacy Policy

Updated October 20, 2026. Applies to Paper Journal (纸帐 · 分页手帐) version 1.0, provided by Yuchen Wang.

Your notes and settings

Handwriting, typed text, images, and imported PDFs are stored in the app's local storage. There is no developer-operated notebook server, advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDK. The app does not require its own account. Appearance, browsing, and eraser preferences remain in the app's local settings.

iCloud

When the device is signed into an Apple account and permits iCloud Drive access, the app uses Apple's iCloud Documents service to back up and synchronize editable documents in your own iCloud storage. The developer cannot access your private documents through this service. You can manage access and storage in system settings. Apple's privacy policy applies to its services. Local editing remains available without iCloud.

Import, paste, and export

The app processes content you choose to create, import, or paste. It does not read the clipboard in the background or automatically share notes with other people. You choose the destinations and recipients of exported, printed, or shared files. Services you choose have their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Notebooks moved to Recently Deleted can be restored. Previous local backups and iCloud revisions may also retain content. Moving a notebook to Recently Deleted does not erase every copy. To remove all data, manage the local app data and delete the app's cloud documents through iCloud storage settings, as well as any copies you have exported or shared. Preserve any backups you wish to keep first.

Diagnostics and contact

The app does not send its own analytics or diagnostic telemetry. Apple's handling of system diagnostics depends on your settings and its policies. If you voluntarily email support, the developer uses the contact details and issue description to respond and resolve the request. Do not send private notes or unnecessary sensitive information. For support or privacy questions, contact jyuchenwang@gmail.com.
